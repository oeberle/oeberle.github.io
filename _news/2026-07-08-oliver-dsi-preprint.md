Our new preprint, coauthored by Maximilian S. Ernst, Lorenz Linhardt, and Aaron Peikert, introduces Distributed Sparse Interventions (DSI), a method for identifying neuron sets whose targeted interventions elicit task-relevant behaviour in instruction‑tuned LMs.
