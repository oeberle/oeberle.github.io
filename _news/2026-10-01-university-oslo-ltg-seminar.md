Gave a talk at the University of Oslo in the Language Technology Group [research seminar](https://www.mn.uio.no/ifi/english/research/groups/ltg/research-seminar).

I presented my research on explainable AI and mechanistic interpretability, exploring how we can understand the inner workings of modern language models from input features and internal representations to feature interactions and algorithmic mechanisms.

Many thanks to the LTG group for having me. Find my slides [here](https://www.mn.uio.no/ifi/english/research/groups/ltg/research-seminar/ltg_oslo_092026_eberle_oliver.pdf).
